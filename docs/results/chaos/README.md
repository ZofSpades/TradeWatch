## Method

- **Load.** `deploy/compose/chaos.yml` runs marketsim at a fixed rate for one session. Corruption is off, so every published event reaches the detectors, and the session is recorded. Abuse is injected ten times as often as in the demo.
- **Faults.** `scripts/chaos.sh` injects the faults mid-stream with Docker: SIGKILL, graceful restarts and rescaling of the consumers. It also re-publishes exact copies of recent records with `chaos duplicate`.
- **Expected alerts.** When the session has finished and every consumer group has committed up to the end of its input, the recording is replayed offline through ingest's normalization and the detection engine, with the same rules (`internal/replay`).
- **Comparison.** Stored alerts are compared with the replay by alert key and by content. Content excludes what legitimately differs between the two: the wall-clock emission and publication times, the detector instance, and the trace.
- **Deliveries.** These come from the alerts topic itself and from the `deliveries` column the sink increments for every redelivery of a key.
